Project Title: Belgut Technical Training Institute (BTTI) Official Website

Objective:
Design and develop a modern, responsive, and user-friendly official website for Belgut Technical Training Institute (BTTI) that showcases academic programmes, campuses, admissions, news, tenders, and institutional information while making it very easy for prospective students to apply for courses online.

1. Branding & Design

Primary Colors:

Green (Education, growth, sustainability)

Gold (Excellence, prestige, success)

Style:

Clean, professional, modern technical institution look

Mobile-first, fully responsive

High accessibility and fast loading

Typography:

Clear, readable fonts suitable for education websites

Imagery:

Use institutional photos, campus images, workshops, laboratories

Include Board of Directors imagery (uploaded separately)
